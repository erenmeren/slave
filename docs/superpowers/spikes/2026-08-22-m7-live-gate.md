- **Deps mode:** the DAG rendered left-to-right with live status borders and attempt counters;

- **Cycle refusal, through the page's own fetch path:** POSTing the reverse edge of a standing
  dependency returned 409 with the exact spec-quoted text; the DAG was unchanged.
- **Completion wave, deterministically replayed:** with an in-page MutationObserver armed (a
  poll-based watcher had missed the 800ms window — observer, not sampling), flipping the
  prerequisite to `done` produced **exactly one `edge-flash` on its outgoing edge**, and the
  dependent immediately read `running` — the scheduler released it in the same breath.
- **The dependency lifecycle end to end, unattended:** the seeded "Draft the essay" (held on
  "Write the outline") started at 16:26:55 — after its prerequisite concluded at 16:26:06 —
  and finished `done`; later the "ballad review" repeated the pattern live under the watcher
  (badge dropped and the run started in the same refetch). Drawing an edge is a real control:
  the scheduler honours it, and completing the prerequisite frees the dependent with no human
  in the loop.
- **Context menus:** real right-click on a task node produced exactly the board and Activity
  deep links; Escape closed the menu **and returned focus to the trigger** (the Task 7 a11y

## Findings

**One cosmetic finding, already ledgered pre-gate:** the org-mode first paint is not fully
fitted — `fitView` runs against the pre-layout node positions, so after ELK resolves, the
bottom of the tree (the satellite) can sit below the fold until the user pans or presses the
fit control. Known as the "fitView-vs-ELK race" in the M7 ledger; recoverable in one click;
carried as a follow-up (fit after the first layout resolve), not a gate-fix task.

Two honest limits of the headless pass, left to human eyes opportunistically: the *feel* of
pan/zoom and particle motion (the DOM proves presence and pathing, not aesthetics), and the
status-flash on a node border was not explicitly captured mid-transition (the mechanism is the
byte-identical M5 idiom whose flash the M5 gate observed live).

## The moral

The gate's by-eyes half does not require human eyes for most of what it checks — a real browser
driven headlessly caught what jsdom structurally cannot (real layout positions, real SSE-driven
DOM changes, a real 800ms animation window) and pinned it with observers instead of sampling.
What remains genuinely human is taste, not truth.
